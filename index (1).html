My dearest Hannah,

There are feelings for which language was never quite built, and yet here I am, attempting to hold the ocean in a teacup. You are the quiet miracle I did not know to ask for, and the one I would choose in every life, in every version of the world.

Your presence is a kind of gentle light. It does not demand attention; it simply makes everything it touches more beautiful. In your company, ordinary hours become treasured ones, and even silence feels like a conversation the heart is fluent in.

I love you without conditions and without ceilings. I love you on the radiant days and on the weary ones, in your laughter and in your stillness, in your strength and in the tender places you let only me see. My devotion is not a season; it is a home I have built for you, and the door is always open.

Thank you for your patience, for your warmth, and for the courage it takes to love someone fully. Thank you for being exactly who you are. Whatever tomorrow brings, I will meet it beside you, grateful beyond words that my heart found yours.

Always, wholly, and forever yours,

💜 Your love
